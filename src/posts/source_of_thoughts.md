Where do thoughts come from? Try to watch your mind for a while and see if you can find the source of your thoughts. They just pops out of nowhere and you have no control over them. What the hell is even going on? Who is the one watching anyway?

The thing is you are not your mind. If you don't pay attention, you might think you are your mind and you will suffer.
