In my experience, the only emotion that can counteract lust is faith. Faith in what? I don't know.
Faith in something. Faith in a higher power, faith in the universe, faith in yourself. What doesn't matter. It is the feeling that is able to counteract lust.

People of every time and civilization knew the cost of giving in to lust. And they must have come across
the feeling of faith that can counteract it. That's why faith always comes up in history.
That could also be a reason why religion is a big part of human history.
